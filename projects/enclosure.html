One-Gallon Stainless-Steel Enclosure Assembly

A mechanical design and engineering documentation project focused on enclosure design, assembly drawings, component specifications, and manufacturing documentation.

Mar. 2023 – Apr. 2023 AutoCAD Mechanical Design

Project Overview

I designed and documented a one-gallon stainless-steel enclosure assembly using AutoCAD. The project required the development of detailed engineering drawings, component specifications, assembly documentation, and a complete bill of materials.


The enclosure was designed using 12-gauge Type 316 stainless steel with a removable panel and corrosion-resistant hardware.

Design Requirements

Material

12-gauge Type 316 stainless steel selected for corrosion resistance and durability.

Assembly

Designed with a removable panel and corrosion-resistant fastening hardware.

Hardware

Included 10-32 PEM nuts, gasket, Schrader valve, pressure gauge, and fastening components.

Engineering Documentation

The project included the creation of:

  • Detailed 2D engineering drawings
  • Orthographic views
  • Isometric views
  • Exploded assembly drawing
  • Bill of materials
  • Item balloons and component identification
  • Material and hardware specifications
  • Dimensions and drawing scales
  • Title blocks and assembly documentation

Design Approach

The enclosure was developed by defining the overall volume and material requirements, laying out the individual sheet-metal components, and documenting how the parts would be assembled.


Particular attention was given to component identification, fastening locations, material specifications, dimensions, and clear assembly communication through engineering drawings.
