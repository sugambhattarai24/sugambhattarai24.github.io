Finite Element Analysis of a Mechanical Key Using Autodesk Inventor

Structural analysis of a 3D mechanical-key model under combined torsional and transverse loading to evaluate stress distribution, deformation, displacement, and factor of safety.

Nov. 2026 Autodesk Inventor Finite Element Analysis Static Stress Analysis

Project Overview

This project involved designing a three-dimensional mechanical key in Autodesk Inventor and evaluating its structural behavior using finite element analysis. The simulation represented loading conditions that can occur when a key is subjected to both twisting and transverse forces during use.


The analysis included material-property assignment, boundary-condition development, combined loading, finite-element mesh refinement, static stress analysis, deformation evaluation, and safety-factor assessment. Critical regions of the model were evaluated to assess structural durability and potential failure locations.

Design & Loading Parameters

3D Mechanical Model

Developed the mechanical-key geometry in Autodesk Inventor using the assigned dimensional requirements while maintaining realistic key proportions and mechanical features.

Torsional Loading

Applied a 10 N·m torque to the key head to represent torsional loading generated while turning a key inside a lock.

Transverse Loading

Applied a 100 N side load perpendicular to the key to evaluate bending stresses in combination with the torsional load.

Finite Element Analysis Setup

Structural material properties were assigned to the completed CAD model before defining the simulation constraints and applied loads.


A fixed constraint was applied to the key shank to represent the restrained region of the component. The model was then subjected to simultaneous torsional and transverse loading.


The finite-element mesh was refined around geometric features and regions where stress concentrations were expected. This allowed greater resolution in areas most important to the structural evaluation.

Analysis Workflow

1. CAD Modeling

Built and refined the three-dimensional mechanical key geometry in Autodesk Inventor.

2. Material & Constraints

Assigned structural material properties and defined the fixed shank boundary condition.

3. Loads & Mesh

Applied 10 N·m torsional loading and a 100 N transverse load, then refined the finite-element mesh around critical geometry.

4. Structural Evaluation

Evaluated stress, deformation, displacement, safety-factor distributions, and critical structural regions.

Structural Analysis

Autodesk Inventor static stress analysis was used to evaluate the response of the mechanical key under the combined loading condition.


The study examined von Mises stress distribution, total deformation and displacement, and factor-of-safety distribution throughout the component. Regions of elevated stress were identified and interpreted in relation to the key geometry, loading direction, and boundary conditions.


The resulting structural behavior was used to assess the durability of the design and determine where geometry, material selection, or local design modifications could improve mechanical performance.

Engineering Takeaways

The project demonstrated the complete workflow required to move from CAD geometry to a structural finite-element model, including realistic constraints, combined loading, mesh development, and interpretation of simulation results.


It also reinforced the importance of evaluating stress concentrations rather than relying only on overall component geometry when assessing mechanical durability and potential failure.
