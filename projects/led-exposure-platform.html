Project Overview

This project involves the design and development of an adjustable mechanical platform for positioning multiple LED light sources above a multi-well cell-culture plate. The system is intended to provide controlled, repeatable, and mechanically stable illumination for photobiomodulation experiments involving endothelial cells.


The design was first developed as a detailed 2D AutoCAD layout defining the base, support structure, adjustable LED carrier, LED-retaining geometry, sample-platform location, and vertical adjustment mechanism. The design was then translated into SOLIDWORKS to develop three-dimensional components and the complete mechanical assembly for fit, clearance, and manufacturability evaluation.

Design Objectives

Adjustable Positioning

Allow the LED carrier to move vertically so the source-to-sample distance can be adjusted and reproduced between experiments.

LED Alignment

Position multiple LED sources directly above selected wells while maintaining consistent mechanical alignment across the plate.

Stable Sample Placement

Provide a defined platform for the multi-well plate so its location remains consistent relative to the illumination system.

AutoCAD Design Development

The first design phase was completed in AutoCAD. The layout establishes the overall geometry of the system and defines the relationship between the base, vertical supports, adjustable carrier, LED locations, and multi-well plate.


Orthographic and sectional views were developed to define component dimensions, support geometry, adjustment features, LED locations, and mechanical interfaces before transitioning the design into SOLIDWORKS for three-dimensional modeling and assembly development.

SOLIDWORKS Design Development

Following completion of the 2D AutoCAD layout, the design was translated into SOLIDWORKS to create individual three-dimensional components and evaluate the complete mechanical assembly.


The SOLIDWORKS models were used to evaluate component geometry, fit, clearances, LED-holder positioning, vertical adjustment features, and relationships between the supporting structure, LED carrier, and sample platform.


Assembly modeling also provided an opportunity to identify potential interference and manufacturability concerns before moving toward physical fabrication and experimental testing.
